Webhooks are planned. They will push the same records the
[changes feed](/datasets/changes) returns (programs added, dropped or changing
phase), signed so you can verify them. Until then, poll `GET /v1/changes`
with `since`; see [Track pipeline changes](/guides/track-pipeline-changes).
