| Dataset | Contents | Id | Links to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Programs](/datasets/programs) | Drugs in development, by company and indication, with phase, modality, target and status | `program_id` | `company_id`, `drug_id`, `nct_id` |
| [Drugs](/datasets/drugs) | Products under their INN, research codes and brand names, with every company developing them | `drug_id` | `company_id` |
| [Deals](/datasets/deals) | M&A, licensing, collaborations, options and joint ventures, with terms and sources | `deal_id` | `company_id`, `drug_id` |
| [Patents](/datasets/patents) | US patent families with owners, members and FDA-listed drugs | `family_id` | `company_id` |
| [Companies](/datasets/companies) | Biopharma companies with tickers, websites and owners | `company_id` | `parent_company_id` |
| [Global Trials](/datasets/clinical-trials) | ClinicalTrials.gov studies with sponsors and linked programs | `nct_id` | `company_id`, `program_id` |
| [FDA Records](#fda-records) | Approvals, applications, orphan designations, letters, advisory meetings, recalls, and Orange Book and Purple Book listings | `record_key` | `application_key`, `company_id` |

Responses carry these ids. A program lists its trials by `nct_id`, for
example, and `GET /v1/trials/{nct_id}` returns each of them.

## FDA records

Each FDA record type has its own endpoint. Records are keyed by FDA
identifiers and linked to a Pav company where possible. `from` and `to` filter
on the date in the second column.

| Record type | `from`/`to` date | `status` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Accelerated Approvals](/datasets/fda/accelerated-approvals) | Approval | `ongoing`, `verified`, `withdrawn` |
| [Advisory Committee Meetings](/datasets/fda/advisory-committee-meetings) | Meeting | `held`, `scheduled` |
| [Complete Response Letters](/datasets/fda/complete-response-letters) | Letter | `Approved`, `Unapproved` |
| [Drug Applications](/datasets/fda/drug-applications) (NDA, ANDA, BLA) | First approval | `approved`, … |
| [Orange Book Exclusivities](/datasets/fda/orange-book-exclusivities) | Exclusivity expiry | Exclusivity code |
| [Orange Book Patents](/datasets/fda/orange-book-patents) | Patent expiry | `listed` |
| [Orange Book Products](/datasets/fda/orange-book-products) | Approval | `RX`, `OTC`, `DISCN` |
| [Orphan Designations](/datasets/fda/orphan-designations) | Designation | `Designated`, … |
| [Purple Book Products](/datasets/fda/purple-book-products) | License | Marketing status (`Rx`, …) |
| [Recalls](/datasets/fda/recalls) | Recall | `active`, … |
| [Warning Letters](/datasets/fda/warning-letters) | Letter | `issued`, … |

[Drug Application](/datasets/fda/drug-application) returns an application
with every FDA record on it and its submissions.
