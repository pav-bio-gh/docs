| Dataset | Grain | Joins on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Programs](/datasets/programs) | One drug in one indication at one company | `program_id`, `company_id`, `drug_id`, `nct_id` |
| [Drugs](/datasets/drugs) | One product across every company | `drug_id`, `company_id` |
| [Companies](/datasets/companies) | One company; owners via `parent_company_id` | `company_id` |
| [Clinical trials](/datasets/clinical-trials) | One ClinicalTrials.gov study | `nct_id`, `company_id`, `program_id` |
| [Deals](/datasets/deals) | One transaction | `deal_id`, `company_id`, `drug_id` |
| [Patents](/datasets/patents) | One US patent family | `family_id`, `company_id` |
| [FDA records](/datasets/fda) | One FDA record | `record_key`, `application_key`, `company_id` |

The ids in the last column appear in responses. Use them to move from one
dataset to another, for example from a program to its trials by `nct_id`.
