| Dataset | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Programs, drugs | Company pipelines, filings and announcements; ClinicalTrials.gov |
| Companies | Company pipelines, ClinicalTrials.gov sponsors, SEC, USPTO, FDA |
| Clinical trials | ClinicalTrials.gov |
| Deals | SEC filings and company press releases |
| Patents | USPTO |
| FDA records | Orange Book and Purple Book (current monthly editions), orphan designations, Drugs@FDA, letters, approvals, meetings, recalls |
| Ontology terms | MONDO, NCIT, HGNC |

Every record links to its source: `source_url`, `nct_url`, deal source
documents, or patent and FDA numbers.

## Fields that are not always set

A filter on a field returns only records where that field is set. Some fields
are set on only part of the data:

- `country`: about a fifth of companies with an active program have one, so a
  `country` filter misses many companies. `company_type` is set on every
  company.
- FDA company links: most Orange Book, Purple Book and orphan records are
  linked to a company. Many warning letters and recalls are not.
