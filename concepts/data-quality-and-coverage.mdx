---
title: "Data Quality and Coverage"
description: "Sourcing, normalization, and coverage posture for Pav data."
---

Pav data is aggregated and normalized from public sources. This page states the
coverage posture plainly so you can reason about what a filter or facet will and
will not return.

## Sourcing

- **Programs / companies** — scraped from company pipeline pages, then
  normalized into canonical phase and modality vocabularies at load time.
- **Clinical trials** — ClinicalTrials.gov records served from Pav's product
  trial index, with Pav program/company links attached where present.
- **Deals** — extracted from SEC filings and company press releases. Each deal
  keeps its source documents, so every term can be traced to a filing or release.
- **Patents** — US applications and grants from the USPTO, grouped into families
  (a root application with its continuations, divisionals and provisionals).
- **Ontology terms** — program indication text is resolved to MONDO terms where
  possible.

## Field coverage

Some fields are sparsely populated at the source. A filter on a sparse field is
honest but narrowing: it can only return rows where that field is present, so
**filtering on a sparse field silently shrinks your result set** to the covered
subset. Measured on the program corpus (2026-06-22):

| Field | Populated | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `phase_norm` | ~97% | Normalized phase; a small tail of unrecognized labels stays null. |
| `modality_norm` | ~81% | ~19% of programs have no canonical modality and are invisible to a `modality` filter. |
| `target` | ~45% | Many programs publish no target; a `target` filter excludes the majority of the corpus. |
| MONDO `indication_terms` | ~16% of programs | Ontology resolution is partial; most programs have no MONDO term yet. |

Phase and modality use closed, normalized vocabularies (pass canonical tokens,
e.g. `small_molecule`). A handful of programs carry `drug = "Undisclosed"` where
the asset is not named.

### Deals

- Many deals disclose no value. `min_value_usd` only matches deals with a stated
  **USD** headline value, so it excludes undisclosed and non-USD deals.
- `companies` lists the parties Pav has resolved to tracked companies. A party
  Pav does not track is named in the deal text but is not in `companies`.

### Patents

- Ownership comes from USPTO assignment records. About a quarter of families
  map to a tracked Pav company, so **no patents for a company does not mean the
  company holds none** — its assignee name may not be mapped yet.
- `statutory_term_end` is the earliest nonprovisional filing plus 20 years. It
  does not apply patent term adjustments or extensions.

<Note>
These rates drift as scrapers and enrichment run. Treat them as orders of
magnitude, not contract guarantees — verify current coverage against the live
API.
</Note>

## Checking coverage yourself

`GET /v1/stats` returns live totals and `by_phase` / `by_modality` /
`distinct_*` breakdowns — a quick way to see facet population without scanning
pages. On deals and patents, pass `include_total=true` to get the exact match
count for a filter.

## Freshness and changes

Programs refresh on Pav's scrape cadence; `source_scraped_at` and
`last_scraped_at` carry the per-row timestamps. Deals update as new filings and
releases arrive; `latest_update_at` and `activity_date` show the latest source.
The [changes feed](/concepts/entities#changes) records detected pipeline changes
(phase moves, added/removed programs) so you can track movement rather than
re-diffing snapshots yourself.
